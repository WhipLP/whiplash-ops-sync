About

Whiplash Ops Sync is a private integration built and operated by Whiplash Merchandising solely for its own internal business use.

It connects Whiplash's internal operations dashboard with Whiplash's own QuickBooks Online company to support bookkeeping and reconciliation workflows, including reconciliation of vendor invoices and payments.

This application is not offered to the public. There is no public signup process, no multi-tenant service, and no third-party customer access.
