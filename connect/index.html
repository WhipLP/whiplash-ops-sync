Whiplash Ops Sync is a private internal integration operated solely by Whiplash Merchandising. It is not available for public signup or use by third parties.

Authorization of the integration is initiated by an authorized Whiplash administrator from the company's internal application. The authorization process uses Intuit's OAuth 2.0 service to connect Whiplash Ops Sync to Whiplash's own QuickBooks Online company.
